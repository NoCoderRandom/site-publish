HIGHCisco Catalyst 2960-S security support has ended

What the vendor says

Cisco’s product announcement gives 5 November 2018 as the end of vulnerability/security support and 30 November 2020 as the last date of support for the 2960-S family. Cisco lists the 2960S-24TS-L among retired models.

What this means

A newly discovered flaw in an unsupported switch may have no vendor security fix. That matters especially when management access is reachable across the network. This support status alone does not prove that this example device has a particular vulnerability or is exposed to the internet.

What to do

  1. Confirm the exact part number, software image and management reachability.
  2. Restrict management to a trusted administration network and disable unused services.
  3. Plan replacement with a supported switch; use segmentation and monitoring until it is removed.

Cisco 2960-S support page · Cisco lifecycle announcement
